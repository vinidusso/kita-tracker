💡 Practical Guide for Munich Kita Applications (Ü3 - Kindergarten)

1. Central Application System

The primary registration must be done via the official portal kita finder+.

The main matching window for children starting in September runs from January to March of the start year (i.e. early 2027). You can select up to high-priority Kitas in your ranking.

2. Quality vs Proximity

For young children (age 3), a daily commute under 15 min walking or 5-7 min biking drastically improves daily routine and sibling logistics.

Look out for "Tag der offenen Tür" (Open Days) in autumn 2026 / winter 2027 to assess staff stability, bilingual programs, food quality, and outdoor garden areas.

3. Fees in Munich

Kindergarten (Ü3) in Städtisch and Münchner Förderformel facilities has traditionally been tuition-free (thanks to Bavarian subsidies), with parents only paying for warm lunch (~€105-120/month).

Even with Bavarian subsidy adjustments scheduled around 2027, municipal and church Kitas remain the most cost-effective options.
